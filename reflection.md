# Student Project Reflection Framework

This document outlines the structured reflection framework for the Canteen Food Demand & Waste Tracker project. In adherence to academic integrity guidelines, the personal experiential reflections must be authored independently by the student (Mo Azruddin Khan). No simulated experiences or ghostwritten feelings are generated here.

---

## 1. Fieldwork Experience
This section is designated for the student's personal account of conducting on-site fieldwork at the Campus Canteen on 17 November 2026. The reflection covers direct interaction with counter staff member Rahul, observing daily customer queues, and analyzing the manual ledger records kept at the counter.

---

## 2. Real-World Constraints Observed
This section focuses on the operational realities encountered during the field visit, including food perishability, rapid shifts in student turnout between class breaks, and the practical challenges small food businesses face when attempting to forecast daily demand without automated tools.

---

## 3. Technical Implementation Learnings
This section covers the software engineering insights gained while building a beginner-friendly data application using Python, Streamlit, Pandas, and Matplotlib. It evaluates the design choices made to ensure data validation, avoid overengineering, and present intuitive metrics and charts for non-technical canteen operators.

---

## 4. Evaluation of Solution Impact
This section addresses how data-driven preparation planning can practically influence daily cooking quantities. It examines whether simple statistical averages provide sufficient baseline guidance to prevent over-preparation of high-demand items like Biryani and Chicken Roll.

---

## 5. Future Scope and Enhancements
This section outlines potential future extensions for the project, such as integrating campus academic calendars, tracking weather patterns that affect footfall, and expanding the tracker to cover additional menu items across multiple counters.
